Jupiter

Jupiter is the biggest planet: It is so large that more than 1300 Earths could fit inside it.

Jupiter rotates very fast: One day = about 10 hours. This makes it the fastest-spinning planet.

  • Jupiter rotates very fast: ~24.6 hours
  • A year on Mars is longer: 687 Earth days.
  • Jupiter has a very long year. One orbit = about 12 Earth years
  • Jupiter has many moons (90+ known).
  • Ganymede is the largest moon.

  • Jupiter has strong gravity: About 2.5 times Earth’s gravity.
  • Jupiter produces strong magnetic fields. The strongest among all planets
  • Jupiter is mostly made of hydrogen and helium
