Venus

Venus is the 2nd planet from the Sun and is often called Earth’s twin because of its similar size and structure. However, its conditions are extremely harsh. It is a terrestrial planet (TP).

Space missions have explored Venus: Like Venera

  • Venus is the hottest planet: Surface temperature ≈ 465°C.
  • Venus is covered with clouds of sulfuric acid.
  • Venus is very bright in the sky: Called the “Morning Star” or “Evening Star”.

  • Venus has a very thick atmosphere: Mostly carbon dioxide (CO₂).
  • Venus has a strong greenhouse effect.
  • Venus rotates backwards: This is called Retrograde Rotation.
  • The Sun rises in the west on Venus.
  • Venus has no moons and no rings.
