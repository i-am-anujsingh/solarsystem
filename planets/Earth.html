Earth

Earth is the 3rd planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. It is a terrestrial planet (TP), meaning it has a solid, rocky surface.

Earth has one natural satellite (moon): Moon

  • Earth is slightly tilted: Tilt ≈ 23.5°.
  • Earth is not a perfect sphere: It is an Oblate Spheroid.
  • Earth has plate tectonics.
  • Earth has different layers: Crust → Mantle → Core.
  • Earth’s core is extremely hot. Temperature ≈ 5000–6000°C
  • Earth has a perfect distance from the Sun: Habitable Zone.

  • Earth is about 4.5 billion years old.
  • Earth’s atmosphere has different layers: Troposphere → Stratosphere → Mesosphere → Thermosphere → Exosphere.
  • Earth’s tilt changes slowly over time: This affects long-term climate called Milankovitch Cycles.
