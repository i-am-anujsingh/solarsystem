Uranus

Uranus is the 7th planet from the Sun and is known for its unusual tilt. It is an ice giant (IG).

Uranus rotates on its side: Tilt ≈ 98°. It looks like it is rolling instead of spinning

Uranus was the first planet discovered using a telescope. Discovered by William Herschel in 1781.

  • Uranus is very cold: Temperature can go below −224°C.
  • Major moons of Uranus include: Titania, Oberon, Umbriel, Ariel, Miranda
  • Uranus has extreme seasons: Each pole gets 42 years of sunlight followed by 42 years of darkness.
  • Only one spacecraft has visited Uranus: Voyager 2.
