Mercury

It is the 1st planet in the solar system.

A year on Mercury is very short: One orbit = 88 Earth days

  • One full rotation = 59 Earth days
  • One full sunrise-to-sunrise cycle = 176 Earth days
  • Mercury has almost no atmosphere: Instead, it has a very thin layer called Exosphere.
  • Mercury shrinks over time: As its core cools, the planet slowly contracts.

Mercury can be seen from Earth
