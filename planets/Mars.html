Mars

Mars has the largest volcano in the solar system: Olympus Mons, About 3 times taller than Mount Everest

Mars has two small moons: Phobos and Deimos

  • A day on Mars is similar to Earth: ~24.6 hours
  • A year on Mars is longer: 687 Earth days.
  • Mars has a giant canyon system
  • Mars has seasons like Earth.
  • Mars sunsets are blue

Mars is the 4th planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance. The color comes from iron oxide (rust) on its surface.
