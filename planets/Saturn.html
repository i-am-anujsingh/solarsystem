Saturn

Saturn is the 6th planet from the Sun and is best known for its beautiful ring system. Like Jupiter, it is a gas giant (GG).

Saturn has the most visible rings: Made of ice, rock, and dust particles. These rings are divided into many smaller ring sections.

  • A day on Saturn is very short: About 10.7 hours.
  • Saturn has many moons (140+ known)
  • Saturn’s largest moon is Titan.
  • Saturn emits its own heat.
  • Saturn is the least dense planet.
