Our Sun

The Sun contains 99.86% of all mass in the solar system.

It is made of plasma (superheated charged particles).

  • The Sun’s gravity is 28 times stronger than Earth’s
  • The Sun produces energy through Nuclear Fusion.
  • The Sun’s surface (~5,500°C) is cooler than its outer atmosphere (corona), which can reach 1–3 million °C.

You could fit about 1.3 million Earths inside it.
