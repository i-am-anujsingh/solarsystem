Solar System

Sun Mercury Venus Earth Mars Jupiter Saturn Uranus Neptune

Stars vs. Sand: Astronomers estimate there are 200 billion trillion stars in the observable universe—far more than all the grains of sand on Earth’s beaches

Our Solar System is part of a galaxy: Milky Way Galaxy.

Cosmic Speed Limit: Nothing can travel faster than the speed of light, which is about 300,000 km per second. That means light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth.

Dark Mystery: About 95% of the universe is made up of dark matter and dark energy, which we cannot see directly but know exists because of its gravitational effects.

Near a black hole, time slows down (this is called gravitational time dilation).
